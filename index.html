Our Story

From Henry's Depot to College Park

Salvatore's Prime Sandwiches first won its following inside Henry's Depot in downtown Sanford, then opened its own College Park restaurant on Edgewater Drive on February 7, 2025. The formula never changed: Italian-inspired sandwiches made with only Boar's Head meats and cheeses, sliced to order and piled high.

  • ✓

    Boar's Head only — every meat and cheese on the board, sliced fresh on site.

  • ✓

    Your way — toasted, open-faced, panini or classic, or build your own.

  • ✓

    Lighter options too — Italian-style salads made with organic greens, plus gluten-free and carb-conscious choices.
